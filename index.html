iPhone widget

Stack value, today's move in dollars and percent, and spot prices on your home screen. Tap it to open a mini app where you can add coins and bullion right on your phone.

iOS
  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store. It's free.
  2. Tap Copy widget script. In Scriptable, tap +, paste, name it Bullion Desk, and tap Done.
  3. Long-press your home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and pick Scriptable. Add it, long-press it, tap Edit Widget, and set Script to Bullion Desk.
  4. Tap the widget any time to manage your stack on your phone.
Moved things around on this page?

Tap Copy stack, open the widget's mini app, and tap Import from website.

iOS refreshes the widget about every 5–15 minutes. A ticking "updated" clock on the widget shows how fresh the numbers are.

Your stack stays in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
