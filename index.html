iPhone home-screen widget

Your stack value, today's move in dollars and percent, and gold and silver spot.

iOS
  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store. It's free.
  2. Tap Copy widget script below. In Scriptable, tap +, paste, and name it Bullion Desk.
  3. Long-press your home screen, tap +, add a Scriptable widget (small, medium or large), then tap it and choose Bullion Desk as the Script.
  4. When your stack changes, copy the new parameter below into the widget's Parameter field. You don't need to paste the script again.
Parameter—

Also works as a Lock Screen widget. It asks iOS for a refresh every 5 minutes, and iOS decides the actual timing (often 5–15 minutes). The "updated" clock on the widget keeps ticking between refreshes. Tap the widget to open this page for second-by-second prices.

Your stack stays in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Live spot: gold-api.com · Fallback & FX: fawazahmed0/exchange-api (daily) Melt values are metal content only. Numismatic premiums are not included.
